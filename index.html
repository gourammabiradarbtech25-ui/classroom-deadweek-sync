What Classroom Deadweek Sync Does

Classroom Sync

Synchronizes your courses and assignments from Google Classroom.

Deadweek Detection

Identifies weeks where several important deadlines are close together.

Smart Workload Balancer

Creates a plan to help you manage your assignments before their deadlines.

Peer Visibility

Allows students to see whether classmates are also stuck on an assignment.

How It Works

  1. Connect your Google Classroom account.
  2. Your courses and assignments are synchronized.
  3. Your deadlines are organized in one place.
  4. Overloaded weeks are detected.
  5. A workload plan is generated.
  6. You can connect with classmates who are stuck.

About Classroom Deadweek Sync

Classroom Deadweek Sync is designed to help students manage scattered academic deadlines and avoid overloaded weeks.
